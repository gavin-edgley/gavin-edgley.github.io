Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

Edgley Home Assistant is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not available to other users.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app reads Gmail messages, creates Gmail drafts, and reads and creates Google Calendar events in the owner's own account.

How data is used

Data is used only to produce the owner's daily briefing, draft replies for the owner to review, and manage the owner's calendar. The app never sends email on its own.

Storage and sharing

Data is processed and stored on the owner's private server. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
